Have questions?

What is ContentMaster and what can it do?

ContentMaster is an AI-powered content creation platform that helps you create professional videos, SEO articles, and social media posts in minutes. It can transform simple ideas, screen recordings, blog posts, or social media content into complete, data-rich content with automated research, images, avatars, and voiceovers.

How do I create my first video with ContentMaster?

Click on 'Create Video', enter your topic or select from available templates. Choose 'Quick mode' and click 'Generate'. Set your video length (short: 1-2 minutes, normal: 3-5 minutes), select language and ratio (Portrait mode recommended for mobile), then click 'Generate now'. ContentMaster handles everything automatically.

What video length and format options are available?

ContentMaster offers two length options: short videos (1-2 minutes) and normal videos (3-5 minutes). For formats, you can choose different ratios, with Portrait mode recommended for optimal viewing on both computers and mobile devices.

What does ContentMaster automatically handle in video creation?

ContentMaster automatically handles scripting, writing dialogue, researching and gathering the latest data, generating videos and images for scenes, creating avatars, voiceovers, and lip-sync. This delivers a complete, professional video ready to share.

How can I customize my avatar appearance and voice?

Go to 'Avatar & Voice' in your dashboard. Browse through hundreds of professional templates with various hairstyles and outfits. Upload a clear photo of your face, and ContentMaster's AI will integrate it with your chosen template. You can also clone your voice by reading provided sample text.

What is the Trending & Viral Ideas feature?

This feature scans social media to find trending conversations in your industry, giving you content ideas that your audience is already interested in. Topics are organized into six categories: Quick Wins, Detailed Execution, Focus Theme, Big Picture Concept, Industry Crossover, and Related Ideas.

How long does it take to create content with ContentMaster?

You can create professional videos in just a few minutes, and complete blog posts or social media content in under 60 seconds. The platform is designed for quick turnaround while maintaining high quality.

Can I translate my content into other languages?

Yes, ContentMaster supports 60 popular languages. Simply click the language icon in any scene to select from the available options. The system automatically adjusts frames to synchronize narration with on-screen action for each language, taking only seconds to complete.

How do I convert existing videos from YouTube, TikTok, or other platforms?

Copy the video link, log into ContentMaster, click 'Create Video', then select 'Extract video's idea' or 'Remix Video'. Paste your URL and click 'Process now'. ContentMaster will analyze and enhance the content with market data, images, and improved scripting.

What is Deep Research and how does it work?

Deep Research automatically searches for and incorporates the latest market data into your content. You can select 'Auto' for ContentMaster to use Google search automatically, or 'Use custom sources' to specify your own data sources. This ensures your content is informative and current.

Can I create SEO-optimized blog posts?

Yes, select 'Write SEO Article' and input your content or video URL. ContentMaster will create comprehensive articles with illustrative images, captions, latest market data, SEO-optimized keywords, and source citations - all ready for publication.

How do I edit individual scenes in my video?

Use the Scene Builder feature. Click on any scene to customize its four overlay layers: text, caption, image, and avatar. You can replace media, adjust duration (recommended 5-8 seconds), add AI-generated images, and modify narration for each scene.

Can I use my own audio or third-party audio services?

Yes, you can use audio from platforms like ElevenLabs or SUNO. Copy the script from ContentMaster, generate your audio using that script, then upload it back to ensure perfect lip-sync with your avatar.

How do I create professional thumbnails?

Click on any video to enter Scene Builder, click the media button of the first scene (designated for thumbnails), select 'AI Generate', enter your desired text, and choose from hundreds of available styles. The thumbnail will automatically use your avatar's face for branding consistency.

What platforms can I source content from?

ContentMaster supports content extraction from YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, and direct file uploads. You can also input your own text, blog posts, or use the trending ideas feature.

Is ContentMaster suitable for creating tutorial videos?

Absolutely. Record your screen silently using any screen recording software, then upload to ContentMaster. It will break the video into scenes, and you can add narration for each scene. This method saves up to 95% of time compared to traditional tutorial creation.

How does ContentMaster help with consistent content creation?

ContentMaster helps you produce content for an entire week in minutes. Use the scheduling feature to set production dates, and the Trending & Viral Ideas section provides daily refreshed content inspiration specific to your field.

Are the generated images and content copyright safe?

Yes, all generated thumbnails and content are unique and copyright safe. Style templates are just references for the AI to understand structure, ensuring your content is original and legally safe to use.

What makes ContentMaster different from manual content creation?

ContentMaster automates the entire process including market research, script writing, image generation, SEO optimization, and source citation. It eliminates hours of manual work while ensuring professional quality and current market data integration.

How much time can I save using ContentMaster?

According to user statistics, 78% of users save at least 2 hours each week. For tutorial creation specifically, you can save up to 95% of time and effort. The platform transforms hours of work into minutes of efficient content creation.
