# Code of Conduct

WideCast adopts the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) for everyone participating in this repo — issues, PRs, discussions.

## Short version

- Be kind. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good intent.
- No harassment, no slurs, no doxxing, no sexual content directed at anyone.
- Disagreement is fine; personal attacks aren't.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@widecast.ai** if you experience or witness behavior that breaks the above. Reports go to the maintainers and are handled confidentially. We won't share your identity without your permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, mute, or ban contributors for violations. Decisions and reasoning are private to the people involved — we don't litigate in public.
